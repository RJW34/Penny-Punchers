# Practice, bots and replay

Training uses the same Core under a visibly labeled training configuration. Each seat independently selects a fighter, bank and mixed rental/EX/super fixture. Applying a fixture does not spend money; it explicitly restores its purchased super use. Health, stun, position, frame advance, speed, reset and checkpoint controls cannot enter competitive recordings or network commands. Display inputs and action frames, hit/hurt/pushboxes, command rejection, combo/advantage, bank, ownership, super use and pending reward status. Actual resolved-contact diagnostics show root, pre-contact outcome, eligible parry age and frozen-edge eligibility; they are observations, not authoritative state.

The 21 drill evaluators cover motion on both facings, charge side-switch, EX chord, link/cancel continuity, target combo, parry levels, red/multihit parry, anti-air, throw tech, kara, quick rise/reversal, confirm into an owned super, denied unowned input, free-kit defense, rollback ownership, zero-bank owned EX, one-use super, counter-hit reward, anti-air reward and precision-parry reward. Existing scenario IDs are retained for compatibility even where their old credit-spend labels changed. Each reset restores its intended loadout, dummy RNG/commands and evaluator state. Checkpoints also restore recording/playback position and lesson progress. Tests exercise failed/no-input cases and actual input sequences; a menu label alone is not drill evidence.

Bots submit normalized InputFrames only. Playable policies use delayed public observations (12 ticks), ownership/remaining uses and a seeded RNG; they do not inspect hidden inputs or change world state. They purchase only options their policy can intentionally execute and make canonically valid mixed carts. Current intentional purchase witnesses cover 21 core-trial and 25 expanded-trial options, a deliberate subset rather than a claim that every passive/branch capability has a strong CPU policy. Training dummy modes and perfect-parry demonstrations remain labeled practice tools. Bot results do not prove human balance.

Replay format 4 stores exact build/content identity, configuration/seed, normalized inputs, quoted preparation commits, every deterministic step event, typed confirmed settlement/use/skill receipts and periodic hashes. Per-round purchases determine super ownership; no free match-selected art is implied by the header. Playback reconstructs the same Core and verifies event, receipt and canonical state equality. Incompatible legacy direct-spend recordings are refused with an explanation and their original files retained. Import is bounded to 64MiB, 120,000 commands, JSON depth 16 and validated collection/string sizes; data is never executable code.

Archives use unique filenames and same-directory temporary writes, durable flush and atomic rename. Completion metadata requires a terminal match decision, not merely a round settlement. Controls provide pause/speed, seek, frame advance, inputs/hitboxes, round economy and a visibly separate training takeover. Every seek restores world, capabilities and receipts together and resets presentation cues. The economy history distinguishes shop cost, saved bank, outcome payout, skill caps, bank clipping and final funds with no next shop. A scrubbed round must match fresh deterministic playback.
