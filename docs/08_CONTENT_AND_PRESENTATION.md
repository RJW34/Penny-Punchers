# Original content production
Rook is a compact motion-input pressure fighter with short-to-mid pokes, pulse projectile, rising elbow, traveling knee and retreat palm. Vale is a longer-ranged charge/spacing fighter with charge pulse, rising heel, turn palm and retreat heel. Neither is a renamed Capcom character. Do not recreate recognizable character appearance, voice lines, stage, music, sprite frames or exact move names/data from commercial games.

Each fighter has 18 standing/crouching/jumping normals; two proximity normals; forward-heavy command normal; universal leap overhead; forward/back throw; four special families with three ordinary strengths and one paid EX each; three alternative supers; six lease-only moves. This totals 49, of which 43 have base availability. Only one of the three supers is usable in a match. All data-defined variants need behavior and coverage, not 49 menu labels for six repeated attacks.

The initial move frames/ranges are original starting data, not balanced final tables. Add distinct pose/hurtbox animation and strength-dependent displacement/projectile speed/recovery. Test each family's job: anti-air, low poke, whiff punish, pressure ender, reversal, throw mixup and approach. Fighter identity must survive zero credits.

## Lease catalog
Two signature options, two forward-heavy replacements and two feints per character. Items reference exactly one fighter/move. A signature adds its unique motion; a technique replaces the base forward-heavy command; a gambit uses back+HP+HK. Cosmetic HP+HK taunt without the back modifier gives no gameplay/economic benefit. There are no charges, tickets or duration-based resource stocks. Equipping does not alter EX/super prices.

## Data implementation details
Fields: move startup/active/recovery, command, price, debit flag, availability, boxes, projectile, throw, movement, invulnerability, cancel windows, special role and superfreeze. Half-open ranges apply everywhere. `movement` assigns vx/vy in milliunits per tick during a segment, integrates that tick without additional gravity, and never adds a second copy of movement. Outside a segment, grounded committed actions have zero self horizontal velocity (collision pushback is separate); airborne actions retain their current velocity and resume gravity. Landing clamps to the floor and clears vertical velocity. Cancel/interruption stops further authored segments. Ordinary movement states use their own walk/dash/jump rules. Do not leave an upward-velocity override active forever. Throws in a `throw` object trigger during the move's active interval even if `kind=special`. Projectiles spawn ONCE on the declared action frame and manage their own contact-group registry/rehit ticks. A projectile-only move with active=1 may continue to have a traveling projectile during recovery. A returning projectile reverses its horizontal velocity once at its own `turn_after_ticks`, preserving the same target-hit registry (it cannot hit the same target a second time unless its authored hit count permits it).

`replaces` disables only that base command while leased; it does not remove ordinary normals or destroy target-combo paths. `availability=base` for EX/supers means known without a shop lease, NOT free activation or permission to use an unselected super. Costs come from canonical move data and must agree with the economy/art definitions.

## Required visual/audio finish
Debug boxes are acceptable early, not as the final fighter presentation. Create original readable humanlike silhouettes using simple 2D rigs/vector parts or original sprites. Each action needs anticipation, active pose and recovery; normal strengths must be visually distinguishable; idle/walk/dash/jump/guard/parry/hit/throw/knockdown/dizzy/win states all need readable presentation. Palette choices must distinguish a mirror match without changing hitboxes. No unseen asset pipeline dependency may block all development.

One original foundry/exhibition arena with a clear floor and corners, plus measurement-grid training view. Consistent UI typography and icons, clear cash numerals and EX/super affordability, parry sparks, hit/block sounds, round feedback, nonintrusive ambience/music and volume controls. Do not require copied samples or a paid generator. Procedural audio and simple original animation are acceptable when intentional and usable, not mislabeled commercial polish.

`assets/ASSET_REGISTER.csv` records source/author/license/created method for every shipped asset. No font files from the assistant environment are provided. Use a compatible system/default or properly licensed project font during implementation; include required notices, not assumed rights. Derived CSVs are regenerated by tools/build_move_tables.py.
