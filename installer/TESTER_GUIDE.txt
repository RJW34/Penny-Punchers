PENNY PUNCHERS 1.0 - TESTER GUIDE

INSTALL AND PLAY
Run Penny-Punchers-1.0-Windows-Setup.exe on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
It installs for your account without administrator access. Godot and the .NET
runtime are included; you do not need to install development tools.

Open Penny Punchers from the Start menu. The optional desktop shortcut is offered
during setup. Choose Versus CPU for a match or Training lab to practice.

Keyboard: WASD to move, U/I/O for punches, J/K/L for kicks.
Menus: arrows, Enter and Esc. Full controls and controller assignment are in
docs/CONTROLS.md and the game's Settings menu.

Vincent is the orange motion fighter. Thomas is the teal charge fighter.
Choose a fighter, stage and trial, then buy the round's equipment in the shop.
Buying nothing keeps your free base moves. Purchased equipment expires each round.

REPORT A PROBLEM
https://github.com/RJW34/Penny-Punchers/issues
Include version 1.0, Windows version, graphics hardware, mode/stage/fighters,
input device, exact reproduction steps, expected behavior and what happened.
Attach a screenshot or replay when useful. Review logs before sharing them.
Godot logs, saved settings and replays are stored beneath:
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Penny Punchers
The included RELEASE_CANDIDATE.json identifies the exact game binaries/content.

UNINSTALL OR REINSTALL
Use Windows Settings > Apps > Penny Punchers, or the Start-menu uninstaller.
Uninstall preserves your Godot user-data folder. Reinstalling 1.0 replaces the
installed game files without resetting those settings and replays.

TEST SCOPE
This installer packages the already verified shop-only v2 game without changing
its mechanics or replay identity. Physical-controller sessions, two physical LAN
PCs and human balance/feel acceptance remain outstanding. See
docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md and docs/UPGRADE_REVIEW.md for the recorded limits.

This installer is unsigned; Windows may display an unknown-publisher warning.
Only obtain it from the project's GitHub release and compare its SHA-256 with the
published checksum file if needed. No project-wide open-source license has been
selected; third-party notices remain included.
