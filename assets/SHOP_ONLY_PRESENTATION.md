# Shop-only v2 presentation

The actual product registry supplies all shop prices, move commands, ownership and
one-use super selection. The two move-library trials both use the shop-only model;
no current menu restores direct combat spending. Historical reports retain their
original meaning and do not certify these changes.

`Main.Purchase.cs` stores stable mixed product IDs. Canonical `PurchasePlanning`
validates an edit before replacing the last-valid cart. Both local plans use quoted
content hash and price before the atomic commit. Three rental slots, each live EX
family and each live super product have semantic owner-specific controls. Save,
load, repeat, keep cash, ready and public-round facts are controller reachable.
Plans store up to six product IDs and are revalidated against the current trial,
prices, bank and category caps. No reserve floor is selectable.

`Main.LineupCatalog.cs` chooses fighters/stage, not a free super. Its untimed
catalog separates rental/EX/super products and includes every runtime action,
including derived branches. Branch practice opens the owning entry action.
`Main.PrepDetails.cs` counts only confirmed public events: observed jumps,
mechanical counter contacts, successful parries, actual allowed skill awards and
super consumption. It records the revealed kit. Counts from one round are not
presented as an inferred habit. The facts popup does not pause the shop timer.

`Main.ArtSkin.cs` shows owned EX, SUPER READY/USED/NONE, the fixed saved bank and
nonspendable next-shop skill credit total. The renderer uses actual SkillAward
amounts; practice-only opportunities are labelled explicitly. Existing normal
parry effects stay independent of whether a reward was earned. No startup money
sound or debit label remains. All existing original character/stage/effect art and
the deterministic fit camera remain in use.

The existing combat showcase was migrated to explicit training equipment and
super-use receipts. A lab loadout is labelled as training; it does not pretend to
be a competitive purchase. The new shop, classification, settlement, rollback and
native acceptance evidence belongs to the v2 release workflow. At this source
handoff, the combined build, new native screenshots and physical/human tests have
not been claimed here.
