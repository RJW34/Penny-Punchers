# User-facing rework
## Shop
Sections: technique slots, four EX family cards (choose up to2), three live super cards (choose0..1). No super chosen for free in lineup. Display costs from the registry, never hardcoded slot price formulas. Cards distinguish ROUND LICENSE/REPEATABLE and SUPER/ONE USE. Explain exact input, replacement, purpose, drawback and ownership expiry. Total cart, bank afterward and last-valid-timeout plan stay visible. Never describe600 license as “two EX attempts.”

Add a compact previous-round strip: opponent's revealed kit, whether a super was used, grounded jump-in/counter/parry observations and public skill awards. Use deterministic match facts, not an LLM inventing a tendency. A small sample can be labeled `2 observed jump-ins`, not `opponent always jumps`. Repeat previous plan is a button that reprices current content and warns about unavailable funds; it does not auto-buy. Suggested plans are explanations, not compulsory counters.

Timed shops should be efficient; full previews/practice live in an untimed catalog and training. Keep readable focus, hotplug, both local seats and remote preparation. Keyboard+controller input routing must not reuse combat MK as pause. Preserve actual current art; add consistent icons/labels rather than creating another entire skin.

Online drafts remain commit/reveal. Local same-screen drafts are visibly shared and both seats edit their own; do not pretend this is private information. Mark this known format difference and test lock-last reactions. A future public-draft parity rule needs a separate decision, not a hidden change here.

## Fight
Primary HUD: health/time/score and purchased capability icons. Show only equipped EX as active; highlight a licensed attempt normally. Super icon READY→USED once. Bank may be shown as a small static “saved for next shop” number or hidden until result, but never as an actionable combat meter. Reward popups say `ANTI-AIR +75 NEXT SHOP`, `COUNTER +50 NEXT SHOP`, `PERFECT +100 NEXT SHOP`; cap/partial amounts must be truthful. Normal parry success has its existing feedback even without money. Reduced-flash/audio settings remain.

No Reserve, LOW FUNDS EX, per-attack money sound, buy prompt mid-combo, or fake XP gauge. Contextual rejection is `EX NOT IN THIS ROUND'S KIT`, `SUPER NOT PURCHASED`, or `SUPER ALREADY USED`. Use icons plus text to distinguish ownership from temporarily illegal execution. Currency terminology must be consistent; skills earn next-period credits, not activatable energy.

## Settlement
Show opening bank; shop spend; saved bank; outcome payout; earned skill by category; awarded vs clipped money; next bank. On match end label there is no next purchase and money resets on rematch. Let player inspect history without adding compulsory lengthy delays. Exports/replay tooltips use the same receipts. Distinguish performance awards from objective combat outcomes: no popup for an event later invalidated by rollback.
