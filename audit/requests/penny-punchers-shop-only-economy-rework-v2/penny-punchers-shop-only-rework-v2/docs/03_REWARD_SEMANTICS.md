# Deterministic skill earnings — exact rules
Credits are earned BY the requested combat achievements but become bank credits only at confirmed round settlement. `PendingSkillCredits` is a nonspendable aggregate of receipts, not an energy meter. No action gate reads it. Local games settle normally at result; network games only after terminal inputs/state are confirmed.

## Proposed values
Counter-hit 50; anti-air 75; precision/perfect parry 100. Maximum two paid awards of each category per player per round and 300 total. A partial final award is allowed to reach the cap: nominal100 with only25 cap space grants25. Track nominal/allowed/capped amounts separately. Values and caps are hypotheses. All earners use the same rule regardless of lead, fighter, equipment, bot/human or round outcome.

## Authoritative facts, not animation labels
Build a `ResolvedContactFacts` record from the simultaneous PRE-CONTACT snapshot and final accepted collision result. This is not just a `Hit` callback and it must not infer ground/air state after the hit launches the defender. Capture root action identity, origin/effective owner, contact ordinal, simulation tick, actor and target prestate, pre-hit combo/hitstun/blockstun/capture/knockdown/dizzy, attack provenance, actual damage and pre-parry damaging threat, parry arm identity/age and frozen-edge flag. Final accepted contacts only: discarded rank candidates, clashes, armor-only absorption and invulnerability misses never earn.

### Counter-hit (CH)
Award on the first damaging opener of a root attack when it interrupts an opponent's committed OFFENSIVE startup. Defender must not already be in hitstun/blockstun/capture/knockdown/dizzy or an ongoing combo. Taunts, passive feints, walking and recovery are not startup for this reward. Do not change the game's existing counter-hit damage multiplier merely to align the reward classifier. Initially direct strikes only: projectile rewards require a later explicit provenance extension. A projectile may still counter-hit mechanically but receives no CH bonus in this candidate.

### Anti-air (AA)
Award the first damaging direct strike of a root attack from a pre-contact grounded attacker against a pre-contact VOLUNTARILY airborne opponent, with no pre-existing hitstun, juggle, knockdown, capture, dizzy or combo continuation. Grounded means feet on floor and no prejump/takeoff state; anti-air status is geometry/state, not a move's marketing name. Hitting a launched combo target, air-to-air hit, projectile, throw or field trigger does not qualify. A successful AA may also be a CH: choose AA75 only, not125.

### Perfect parry (PP)
This is a new reward classification over the existing FREE parry, not a replacement defense mechanic and not a claim of exact Third Strike/SF6 timing. A legal high/low/air/red parry that intercepts the hit on eligible defense-clock ages 0 or1 (first two ticks) qualifies. A later legal parry still blocks damage normally and earns no precision bonus. No extra slowdown, invulnerability, damage, cost or input button is attached.

`EligibleDefenseTick[seat]` advances on fight ticks when the defender is not already in hitstop/global superfreeze and contacts can be evaluated. Record its value on the qualifying fresh neutral→direction edge BEFORE resolving contact; compare the same clock at contact. Do not use render time, OS input milliseconds, raw global Tick, or guessed animation age. A parry edge originally entered while hitstop/superfreeze is active is marked `BonusIneligibleFrozenEdge`; it may retain whatever regular buffered-parry behavior the current game permits, but cannot become a free perfect reward on thaw. A genuinely fresh rearm after thaw can qualify. Keep mirrored/facing/landing and normal parry audits intact. Existing red-parry success will generally meet this short classification if freshly entered; it has no extra category multiplier.

Only a genuinely damaging attack qualifies; a zero-damage cosmetic/fake object does not. Perfect parrying an enemy ordinary/EX/super projectile can qualify if it is an actual accepted manual parry; a purchased auto-counter/reflection/interceptor never does. One reward per opposing ROOT ATTACK (all multihits, generated fields and projectiles inherit the same origin). Subsequent taps on a multihit super may defend but do not farm additional income. Parrying your own reflected original projectile is ineligible. Keep origin ownership across reflection.

## Arbitration and dedupe
Offensive reward key = session/round/earner/own-root-attack; defensive key = session/round/earner/opponent-root-attack. One maximum offensive award per root: if the same contact qualifies CH and AA, select highest nominal first. If that category is capped, do not fall back to a lower category. Consume the root's reward decision even for category/global-cap denial. An early qualifying CH cannot later upgrade itself to AA from the same action. Each projectile or follow-up preserves a declared root; new legal neutral actions get new roots. Use monotonically assigned action ordinals, not move names or event callback counts.

Process a tick's final reward facts in deterministic order: tick, earner, root, contact ordinal. This order is independent of thread arrival, network delivery and rendering. Duplicate event IDs with identical facts are ignored; conflicting facts with the same ID fail closed. Same-tick lethal awards count if earned on the terminal frame; no post-terminal farming. Trades use the pre-contact snapshot for both sides. Receipts/counters/seen roots/precision stamps serialize and rollback with combat.

## Anti-farming boundary
No money for ordinary damage, whiffs, passive waiting, basic block, normal parry, combos, throwing a corpse, dummy training, projectile destruction, or the act of buying. The bonus cap bounds intentional feeding but cannot prove its absence. Stronger players may earn more; reward income is not inherently a comeback mechanic. Test collusion/repeated jump or projectile feeding, early-reward cap races, and scenarios where chasing rewards loses the round. Do not claim this defeats all adversarial strategies.
