# Deliberate exclusions and later skill categories
The requested three rewards are mandatory. Further categories are experiments, not unfinished requirements:

- A true whiff-punish bonus needs pre-contact recovery-state provenance and a measurable whiff, not every hit on someone pressing a button. It would overlap CH/AA and needs a one-opener priority policy.
- Throw-tech income could encourage fishing/feeding techs and requires the same opponent-action cap. Keep the actual tech free regardless.
- A clean-confirm/varied-combo bonus risks paying execution specialists twice and encouraging longer combos over finishing. Do not infer quality from move count.
- A round-win method bonus, low-health comeback bonus or perfect-round reward further pays strong outcomes; evaluate its additional snowball separately before adding.

Do not add income for raw damage, whiffing, basic block, passive waiting, ordinary parry, destroying your own object, or repeated weak dummy behavior. No interest, paid income multiplier, cheaper-shop upgrade, global purchased health/damage/speed, permanent winner-retained inventory, or team financial support in this rework.

Round-long EX access is the primary default. An experimental finite EX-ammo economy or match-long persistent move ownership would be a DIFFERENT gameplay decision. They might eventually be worth testing, but are not stealth substitutions for the repeated round-license implementation. The single prepaid super use is already disclosed in the core rules, not a general model of ammunition for every move.
