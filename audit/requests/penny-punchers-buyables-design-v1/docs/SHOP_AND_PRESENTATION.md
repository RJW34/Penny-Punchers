# Shop, catalog and presentation design

## The player question

"What am I trying to make happen in the next round, and how much will I keep for the fight?"

A shop screen should explain a tactical plan, not simply occupy fifteen seconds between fights. Use the same view model for local and private-network preparation. Preserve separately declared input ownership and draft-information policy. Local visible drafts and online commit/reveal are not identical competitive information conditions; this pack does not silently resolve that outstanding audit decision.

## Layout contract

The top line shows round/score, actual wallet, selected art and its price, actual decision timer, and ready state. The previous-round mini-ledger shows starting funds, rentals, accepted EX/super expenses, nominal/granted/clipped payout, and current funds. All values derive from canonical receipts/content.

The center has three slots with explicit item cards and a prominent Keep Base Kit option. Move details appear on focus; do not hide all alternatives in cycling text. Choose by stable item ID, not array position. Compare added versus replaced commands and preserve controller focus semantically after edits.

The bottom shows **remaining spendable cash after lock** and **optional protected savings** separately as labels on the same wallet, never two bars. Available examples can read "900 left: selected Circuit Break OR three EX attempts." These are alternatives, not additive reservations. Show 600-credits/900-super shortfall plainly.

The footer shows one final plan receipt and a deliberate Ready action. An unaffordable card says why; it never silently commits a different card. At timeout, choose a specified valid fallback and visibly show the exact locked plan before combat. An unlocked invalid draft is not an authorization to debit then repair.

## Every card must show

Name and role; exact rental/activation fee and duration; input with physical-device glyphs; add/replace information; two-sentence tactical purpose; one main drawback; real recovery/contact facts with unmeasured advantage omitted; remaining cash and selected art opportunity cost; practice availability; current roster compatibility.

Do not sell an enhanced attack as an unlock. The combat-funding panel explains EX/super prices and inputs but has no purchase button for tickets. Super selection is free at match setup. No duplicated extra license charge.

## Practice flow

Untimed catalog: choose fighter/opponent/side and actual item, then test versus stand, block, parry, jump, poke and the specific item counter. Compare with original move using one action. Permit credits to be set in training, clearly labeled, with no competitive receipt mutation. Use legal input demonstration and optional ghost input trace rather than teleporting directly to a successful hit.

The competitive shop may play a read-only short preview; it may not stop the other player's timer or enter a live lab. A saved plan re-evaluates prices and availability against current content and shows changes. Do not silently reinterpret a saved plan after a slot/ID migration.

## Readability and polish

Use ordinary names for mechanics and concise flavor, not financial jokes where the player needs input clarity. A role tag such as THROW, ANTI-AIR, FEINT, LAUNCHER or SETUP sits beside the name. Prices share one currency icon. Paid-startup feedback briefly states the move and debit without obscuring health or the opponent.

Feints intentionally share the initial animation of one specific real threat. That deception is gameplay. Do not extend it to fake hit sparks, fake wallet changes, fake network notices or unmarked defensive properties. The opponent knows from reveal that the feint is equipped; they still must identify the action in combat.

Rented replacement normals need visible loadout reminders and distinct motion. Temporary Overtime uses a compact expiring icon/countdown, not an earnable meter. Prism Lattice shows actual space and object phase; two contact pips are the object's remaining collisions, not purchased cast stocks. Both must remain readable with reduced flashes and monochrome-safe shape cues.

No automatic movie-like slow motion outside the simulation's declared freeze rules. Visual cleanup never changes hurtbox timing. Presets and screenshots must show real dynamic values, not baked text from a decorative board.
