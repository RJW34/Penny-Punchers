# Balance is exclusively 1v1
No team budgets, transfers, carry economy, subsidized losing lane, duo compositions, separate-arena timing or team win rates. Every test is two opposing fighters, one arena, two independent scalar wallets. Both Rook/Vale and mirrors must be tested, including each selected-super pairing.

## Questions, not pretend balance verdicts
Does a zero-credit fighter retain satisfying offense and defense? Does an optional lease ever beat keeping the same credits for EX/super? Does an early winner snowball through saved money? Can losing intentionally for recovery tier ever improve expected match win over plausible contested play? Does the rich player chain paid invulnerable reversals or chip strings into a near-forced win? Does high-level parry make all expensive offense unviable? Is a low-cost super always dominant, or does each art's coverage justify its price? Does a lease's command grab remove counterplay? Are players saving from uncertainty instead of choosing intelligently?

Do not solve these risks by introducing a second spendable gauge, combat income, teammate sponsorship, invisible rubber-banding or pay-to-use blocking. Tune cost, wallet cap, income, recovery, attack properties or lease roles, recording tradeoffs. A universal per-round EX token limit would violate the one-wallet/no-ticket design; a legitimate recovery/cancel rule is a combat constraint, not a new currency.

## Controlled experiments
Run mirrored policies with randomized starting credits0/300/600/900/1800/3600, swap seats and fighters, and compare identical frozen match states under counterbalanced budgets. Observational rich-player win rate is confounded by skill and earlier wins. Compare zero-lease versus lease plans at equal total opening cash; costs must be included, not gifted. Stratify by art, matchup, round score, corner position and health lead. Include the top-tier loss bonus states, wallet cap and final-round all-in.

Bots must have documented reaction/input restrictions and seeds; perfect-info training agents are adversaries, not models of ordinary humans. Produce raw per-round records (credits before prep, lease spending, attempted/accepted/rejected EX/supers, credits spent, hit/block/parry/whiff outcomes, damage/stun, result, payout/cap clipping, next cash). Report sample counts, uncertainty intervals and excluded/aborted runs. No Elo or “balanced” claim from a few scripted matches.

## Economic policies
Baseline policies: spend-on-confirm; conservative reserve; force available EX; bank for selected super; lease-focused; zero-spend defense; controlled losing/low-contest stress strategy. Evaluate full-match results, not damage/credit in isolation. Damage efficiency can ignore corner position, knockdown, stun and the round-winning conversion. A zero-cost normal makes a naive ratio undefined; never divide by zero or rank all free moves infinitely optimal.

Full-spend on the last decisive round is expected. A system that punishes rational final-round use with imaginary future debt is not fixed. The design goal is meaningful variation DURING a multi-round match, not equal spending on every round. Final comparison should include player-reported agency/clarity and shop downtime, not only engine telemetry.
