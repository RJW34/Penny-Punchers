# Product contract: Strike Ledger
**Authoritative revision: 2026-09-05. Working title; not a cleared commercial name.**

Build an original, native desktop, traditional 2D fighting game for **exactly two players in one arena**. Third Strike is the preferred qualitative mechanical feel. It is not the engine binary to reuse, a ROM patch, an emulation target, or a claim of exact frame parity. All previous platform-fighter and team-game plans are superseded.

## Non-negotiable finish
A working animated and audible two-character game with complete free six-button kits, directional motion and charge inputs, grounded movement, blocking, high/low/air/red parries, throws and throw techs, hit confirms, cancels, knockdowns, corner pressure, EX variants and three selectable supers per fighter. Paid combat actions debit the same persistent credits used in the between-round shop. No earned EX/super/drive gauge, no purchased super tickets, and no combat-resource farming.

Playable local 1v1 (two controllers or keyboard plus controller), versus bots, training with frame advance and drills, recorded/replayed matches, and private 1v1 LAN/direct-IP rollback. One competitive stage plus a visually distinct training grid with identical fighting geometry. Complete character/super select, preparation, fight, results, rematch, settings, controllers, audio, help, economy tutorial and Windows/Linux export packages. No accounts or paid service required.

The match is up to nine one-health-bar rounds. Five points clinch, a round draw gives half a point each, and higher score wins at the regulation cap. Exact final tie is an honest match draw. This is not a first-to-two game nested inside another best-of-nine. Baseline clocks are 60 combat seconds, 15 preparation seconds, 3 reveal seconds; ready can shorten preparation.

## One economy, not two renamed meters
There is ONE integer credit balance per player. EX and the chosen super cost credits on committed startup. Leased optional techniques cost credits at preparation lock and expire after the round. Health, stun buildup, input charge duration and a round clock are state, not spendable currency. Base movement, blocking, parries, throw tech, normal throws, every normal and all ordinary specials remain free at zero credits.

No shared wallets, gifts, assists, split arenas, relays, team scoring, team balancing, four-seat infrastructure, hidden team toggles or optional team work packages. Do not preserve them for future compatibility. The two players are opponents, never teammates.

## Bounded original content
Rook: forward-pressure motion-input brawler. Vale: charge-oriented spacing/kick fighter. Both have 43 baseline action definitions including 4 EX variants and 3 supers, plus 6 optional leased moves; 49 each. A selected super is locked for the match; only that super is executable. Selection is free. Prices distinguish risk/coverage, not arbitrary meter lengths. Twelve lease items total, two alternatives per slot per character. Do not turn them into stat boosts or mandatory missing base actions.

## Exclusions
Public matchmaking/ranked infrastructure, online accounts, microtransactions, purchased currency, persistent progression, story campaign, large roster, console certification, mobile/web, arcade ROM integration, exact Capcom data, CPU unbeatable frame-perfect reactions, original platform mechanics, and team modes. These exclusions do not excuse unfinished private rollback or basic presentation.

Finish is evidence-backed. Software verification, physical target-device verification and actual human feel acceptance are separate gates. Lack of a human/device may leave that external gate pending; it cannot be claimed as a pass or used to abandon unrelated implementation.
