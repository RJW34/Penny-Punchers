# Controlled 1v1 economy experiments

Run the release executable with `--data data --output reports/balance-lab-candidate --seeds 1`. Scenario filters `zero_vs_full_wallet` and `recovery_farming` are supported; unknown scenarios fail.

One seed runs 252 completed-match experiments: seven policies × nine shared contexts × two budget assignments × two mirrored seats. Every policy sees the same context and adaptive combat seed. Each context reverses the actor/opponent opening budgets while holding fighter, selected arts, seed, position, health and score conditions fixed; it then mirrors the seats. The design covers Rook/Vale and mirrors, all nine art pairings across contexts, and credits 0/300/600/900/1800/3600. It includes corner pressure, health leads, tier-2 recovery, cap and a frozen decisive final round. This is a bounded stratified design, not every factorial combination.

Policies are spend-on-confirm, conservative reserve, force available EX, bank for the selected super, lease-focused, zero-spend defense and controlled losing for the first two rounds. Inputs pass through the production recognizer. Bots never mutate the running simulation. The separate laboratory executable initializes trusted experimental fixtures by reflection before writing their canonical snapshots; this does not add a wallet mutation command to the shipped game or network.

`matches.jsonl` stores context, complete-match result and initial/final hashes. `rounds.jsonl` stores opening credits/recovery/health, lease and combat spending, accepted/rejected paid commands, move starts, hits/blocks/parries, melee whiffs and interruptions, projectile starts, observed stun buildup, payouts, cap clipping and closing credits. Exact initial snapshots are under `frozen-states`. `summary.json` reports sample counts, excluded runs, bounded uncertainty estimates and limitations.

Paid attempts count commands reaching accepted/rejected core transition checks; inputs entered during unactionable frames are not counted as activation attempts. Melee whiffs are distinguished from interruption before contact, while projectile starts/contacts are recorded separately. Positive observed stun changes can undercount a same-tick dizzy reset. Paired seats are correlated, so the reported Wilson intervals are descriptive and not a claim of independent human-performance evidence. No tuning or declaration of competitive balance is inferred automatically.
